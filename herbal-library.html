Herbal Library

Turmeric (Curcuma longa)

Anti-inflammatory Antioxidant Immune Support

Known for its powerful anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Contains curcumin, which has been studied for various health benefits.

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

Adaptogen Stress Relief Energy

An adaptogenic herb that helps the body manage stress. Traditionally used to support energy levels and overall well-being.

Ginger (Zingiber officinale)

Digestive Aid Anti-nausea Anti-inflammatory

Widely used for digestive support and nausea relief. Contains gingerol, a compound with potent anti-inflammatory properties.

Echinacea (Echinacea purpurea)

Immune Support Antiviral Antibacterial

Popular herb for immune system support. Contains compounds that may help fight viral and bacterial infections.
